Scarborough / Mt. Grace District Office — V207

DISTINCT PUBLIC PAGE IDENTITIES

V207 gives each focused public page a clearer visual and functional identity while preserving the same District Office brand.

Page identities:
- /office/ — Office Directory
  Focus: people, leadership and district information.
- /services/ — Service Dashboard
  Focus: finding help, understanding requirements and taking the next step.
- /community/ — Community Hub
  Focus: projects, participation and district activity.
- /forms/ — Task Workspace
  Focus: apply, enquire and track.
- /updates/ — Public Information Desk
  Focus: current activity, published information and progress.
- /contact/ — Help Centre
  Focus: call, WhatsApp, visit and enquiry follow-up.

Visual changes:
- Route-specific civic accent palettes while retaining the navy/blue District Office identity.
- Route-specific header gradients.
- New identity strip at the top of each focused content column.
- Three concise page-focus chips on every route.
- Route accent applied to quick-action cards, section chips, sticky page navigation and content cards.
- Small route badge added to each focused-page hero.
- Subtle route-specific refinements for service cards, forms, community content, updates, office directory content and contact actions.

Responsive behaviour:
- Identity strip collapses cleanly on tablet.
- Explanatory copy is shortened automatically on phones.
- Page-focus chips remain visible on mobile without creating excessive height.

Architecture:
V207 is presentation-only. It does not duplicate content, change enquiry logic, modify staff authentication, or alter the public data source.
