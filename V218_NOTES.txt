Scarborough / Mt. Grace District Office — V218

INTELLIGENT MOBILE NAVIGATION

V218 builds on the screenshot-driven V216/V217 fixes by making the mobile navigation system behave more intelligently rather than only looking better.

1. Mobile primary navigation now centers the current area
- The active Home / Office / Services / Community / Forms / Updates / Contact item is automatically scrolled into the centre of the horizontal navigation rail on load.
- The rail recentres after viewport changes.
- The current item receives a clearer muted-brass active marker.
- Existing previous/next arrow controls and swipe behaviour remain intact.

2. Focused-page tabs now track the section being read
- The horizontal focused-page tab row now behaves as a proper section navigator.
- IntersectionObserver watches the visible page sections.
- As residents scroll, the relevant tab receives an active state.
- The active tab automatically scrolls toward the centre of the horizontal tab rail.
- Hash links and direct tab clicks update the active state immediately.
- Existing section content and deep-link behaviour remain intact.

3. Explore drawer becomes a cleaner mobile accordion
- At <=900px, only one Explore group remains open at a time when the search box is empty.
- The group matching the current route opens automatically:
  /office -> Your District Office
  /services -> Resident Services
  /community -> Community
  /forms -> Forms & Portals
  /updates -> Updates & Information
  /contact -> Contact & Enquiries
- The current group receives a subtle “Current” marker.
- Searching the drawer can still open multiple matching groups so search is not restricted.
- All existing child links remain available.

4. Focused-page masthead is more efficient on mobile/tablet
- The Tobago House of Assembly topbar retains both messages but uses tighter spacing.
- The District Office identity banner is reduced in height on focused subpages.
- Logo, office name and People • Community • Opportunity remain present.
- The homepage keeps its fuller identity treatment.
- On narrow phones the topbar can wrap cleanly instead of clipping.

5. Focused-page tabs
- Horizontal overflow is now intentionally swipeable.
- Scroll snapping is centred rather than leaving the active item clipped at an edge.
- Scrollbars remain hidden visually while keyboard/touch navigation stays available.
