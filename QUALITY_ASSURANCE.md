# Service Quality and Continuous Improvement

## Purpose
The Quality Centre supports service standards, quality-assurance sampling, corrective actions and recurring management review.

## Operating principles
- Targets are management standards, not evidence of actual performance.
- Official measurements must be calculated from the approved production database and audit records.
- QA sampling should avoid copying unnecessary resident personal information into the quality register.
- Corrective actions require an owner, due date, status and completion evidence.
- High-risk findings should be escalated through incident management where appropriate.
- Management should review trends, overdue actions, resident feedback, referral backlogs and training needs on a recurring cycle.

## Recommended cadence
- Weekly: overdue corrective-action review.
- Monthly: service-quality management review.
- Quarterly: service-standard and trend review.
- Annually: standards, retention and operating-procedure review.

## Production requirements
The live system should calculate SLA and quality metrics server-side, preserve audit history, enforce permissions, retain approved evidence and prevent retroactive alteration of finalized review records without an audit event.


## Resident feedback input
Monthly quality review should consider recurring resident-experience themes, unresolved service complaints and overdue service-recovery actions from the Resident Feedback Centre.
