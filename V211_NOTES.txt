Scarborough / Mt. Grace District Office — V211

HOMEPAGE AESTHETIC BRIDGE

V211 reconnects the public homepage to the V210 civic/editorial language so the landing page and focused subpages feel like one visual system.

Homepage changes:
- Restyles the main hero with layered navy/blue civic depth, muted brass accents, concentric rings and stronger editorial typography.
- Adds a small "District Office • Public Service" masthead detail beneath the hero quote.
- Adds six chapter-style visual transitions through the homepage:
  01 Resident access
  02 Service directory
  03 District life
  04 Office access
  05 Community record
  06 Explore more
- Adds two prominent homepage gateway cards:
  * Your District Office -> /office/
  * Community Action -> /community/
- Uses civic medallions, fine rules, radial backgrounds and constellation details to connect those gateways visually to V210.
- Restyles Resident Start, Portal Hub, Activity Hub, Contact, Service Forms, Gallery, District Office and Community Action sections so they share the same material language.
- Gives the Activity Hub and Gallery deeper navy editorial framing.
- Strengthens the hidden/expanded District Office section with framed office imagery and a softer editorial information panel.
- Restores visible constellation linework throughout the homepage.
- Restyles the More gateway as a deliberate final chapter rather than a generic reveal control.
- Adds small decorative folio numbers to major homepage chapters.

Design principles:
- Navy/blue remains the dominant District Office identity.
- Metallic detail stays muted brass/champagne rather than bright yellow.
- Decorative devices are secondary to service information and navigation.
- Mobile removes or reduces excess rings, folio numbers and ornament.
- No public content, forms, enquiry logic, tracking logic, staff authentication or database behaviour is changed.
