# User Acceptance Test Plan

Use the Staff Portal UAT Centre to record Pass, Fail or Blocked results. Test with fictional data only until the production environment is formally approved.

Minimum acceptance areas: public enquiry and tracking, case assignment, agency referral, resident-safe tracking, appointments, community matters, town halls/projects, records sensitivity, website publishing, reports, role permissions, audit events, backup/restore, mobile/accessibility, and outage/rollback handling.

Go-live requires management sign-off after critical workflows pass and critical/high issues are resolved or formally accepted with documented mitigation.
