Scarborough / Mt. Grace District Office — V214

FEED PUBLISHING, PINNING & RETURN-VISIT AWARENESS

V214 deepens the District Digital Feed into a real public-information publishing system without removing or replacing the full source sections introduced before V213.

Public feed enhancements:
- Staff-managed public content can now be matched back to its real public-content record.
- Featured Activity Hub items appear as PINNED in the District Digital Feed.
- Pinned items stay at the top of the feed.
- Published/updated dates from verified public-content records are added where the feed did not already have a date.
- No date is invented for static content that does not have one.
- Feed items with managed public-content IDs receive stable permalink URLs using:
  /updates/?post=<public-content-id>
  or the equivalent Community route.
- Opening a permalink highlights and scrolls to the exact feed item.
- Copy Link and Share use the exact managed-item permalink when one exists.
- Static items continue using their full section URL.

New-since-last-visit:
- The browser records the visitor’s most recent District Digital Feed visit locally.
- On a later visit, managed items published or updated after that timestamp receive a NEW badge.
- A compact banner reports how many new public items are present.
- First-time visitors are not told that the entire archive is “new”.
- This uses browser local storage only; no resident account or personal profile is created.

Staff CMS:
- Activity Hub editor now includes:
  “Pin in District Digital Feed — Yes / No”
- The control maps to the existing public_content.is_featured field.
- Existing verification and workflow rules still apply.
- An item must still be Published + Verified before it is public.
- Managed Activity Hub register now shows whether each record is Pinned or Standard.
- Editing an existing item restores its current pin state.
- Clearing the editor resets pin state to No.
- Built-in Activity Hub overrides can also be featured through the same controlled workflow.

RSS:
- Adds /api/public/feed.xml.
- RSS contains only database-managed content that is already Published + Verified and within its publication window.
- Hidden Activity Hub overrides are excluded.
- Feed items link to stable public update permalinks.
- The public HTML advertises the RSS endpoint through a rel="alternate" discovery link.
- RSS does not expose private cases, resident records, internal notes or unverified drafts.

Information integrity:
- Full original source sections remain unchanged and available.
- Pinning changes prominence only; it does not alter wording or status.
- NEW is based on the record’s real publish/update timestamp.
- Static legacy content without a managed timestamp is never falsely marked New.
- Existing status language remains authoritative.
