# Events, Town Halls & Volunteer Operations

## Purpose
This module manages the operational execution of town halls, stakeholder roundtables, community projects, volunteer activities, youth programmes and outreach events.

## Core workflow
1. Create the event and assign a lead officer.
2. Link it to an initiative, project, community matter or other office reference where applicable.
3. Record venue, date, purpose, expected attendance and internal planning notes.
4. Maintain staff-only attendance, volunteer and partner participation registers.
5. After the event, record actual attendance and an approved resident-safe outcome summary.
6. Create action items with an owner and due date.
7. Transfer longer-running actions to the appropriate case, community matter, initiative, application or agency-referral workflow.
8. Publish only separately approved public summaries through the Website CMS / public engagement workflow.

## Privacy
Attendance names, volunteer contact information, partner contacts and internal planning notes are operational records and must not be published automatically.

## Production requirements
Production should use authenticated staff accounts, central database storage, audit events, controlled event IDs, secure attendance storage and permissions by role.
