Digital Services Public Guide

How to use the District Office website, enquiry service and follow-up tools.

1. Find the right service pathway

Use the Resident Service Portals or the service information on the main website. If you are unsure, submit a general enquiry.

2. Submit an enquiry

Provide enough information for staff to understand the request. A successful live submission returns an official District Office reference. If no reference is returned, the submission has not been confirmed as received.

3. Keep your reference

Use the reference when contacting the office or using the resident tracker. The public tracker shows only approved resident-safe status information.

4. Documents and privacy

Do not send sensitive identification or personal documents through unsecured channels. Secure document upload is available only when the production private-storage service is enabled.

5. Applications and referrals

The District Office can assist with guidance, document checklists, referrals and follow-up. Eligibility and approval decisions are made by the responsible agency or programme.

6. Feedback

Residents may raise a service concern, suggestion, compliment or accessibility issue. A complaint should not disadvantage anyone seeking assistance.
