# Field Visits & Outreach Operations

Field work should be linked to an operational record wherever possible. Record only the minimum information necessary for the service task. Browser-training mode stores metadata locally and must not contain confidential photographs or resident documents.

## Recommended workflow

1. Plan or confirm the visit.
2. Link it to a case, application, community matter, initiative/project or partner record.
3. Record the lead officer, location, purpose and date/time.
4. Record factual observations after the visit.
5. Record the outcome and next action.
6. Set a follow-up date when further action is required.
7. Register supporting evidence in the Records Centre.
8. Publish a resident/public update only through the approved public-content workflow.

## Production requirements

Production should support authenticated staff identity, server-side field records, secure evidence storage, audit history, offline-safe mobile capture, role permissions, and sync/retry controls.
