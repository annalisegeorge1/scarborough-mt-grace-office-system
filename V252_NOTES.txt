Scarborough / Mt. Grace District Office — V252

FOCUSED CASE MANAGEMENT CLEANUP

Reason
A deployed tablet screenshot showed the new shared Residents navigation above multiple legacy navigation systems and launch-readiness cards. The result was visually chaotic even though the underlying V250/V251 architecture was already present.

Changes
1. Case Management now treats the V250 shared staff shell as the only global navigation.
2. Legacy District Office Staff System navigation is visually removed from the Case page.
3. The old internal case-portal topbar and module sidebar are visually removed.
4. Production, Go-Live, Training, UAT, Pilot, Website Management, Attention Centre and other embedded dashboards are removed from the Case page's visual flow; their dedicated pages remain intact.
5. A compact Case Queues bar now exposes All, My Cases, Overdue, Urgent/High and status filtering.
6. The existing four case metrics, search/filter toolbar and case register become the primary Case Management surface.
7. V251 case tabs remain the detailed record workspace after a case is opened.
8. Mobile/tablet contextual tabs now have stronger contrast and clearer active state.
9. Backend release identity advanced to 252.0.0.
10. Preflight/smoke coverage includes the new focused-case assets.

No database migration is required.
