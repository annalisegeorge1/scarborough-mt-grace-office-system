# District Office System — Operations Handbook

## Daily opening
1. Sign in using your individual account.
2. Review the Attention Centre, overdue follow-ups, appointments and agency responses.
3. Confirm any public notices due to publish or expire.
4. Check system health before entering new production records.

## Case handling
- Create one case per resident matter unless an existing reference should be updated.
- Use the Community Matter Control Centre for broader issues affecting several residents.
- Keep internal notes, documents and resident identities out of public updates.
- Assign an owner, next action and follow-up date whenever work remains outstanding.

## Publishing
- Draft → Review → Approved → Published.
- Verify dates, responsible agency and public wording before publication.
- Use corrections rather than silently rewriting material where the earlier public statement materially changed.

## Records
- Classify each record Public, Internal or Restricted.
- Public publication is a separate approval decision from document registration.
- Follow approved retention and disposal rules.

## Meetings, projects and partnerships
- Maintain one structured initiative record.
- Add timeline updates instead of overwriting history.
- Convert outcomes into assigned action items with due dates.

## Security
- Never share accounts or passwords.
- Do not put real resident data in browser-only training storage.
- Report suspected unauthorized access or data exposure immediately.
- Production permissions must be enforced by the server.

## Closing routine
1. Update outstanding cases and follow-up dates.
2. Confirm same-day communications are logged.
3. Review unresolved urgent matters.
4. Verify backups/system status according to the production schedule.


## Staff availability and continuity
At the start of each week, verify the duty roster and expected absences. For each absence requiring coverage, assign a covering officer and identify the responsibilities being covered. Before a staff member becomes unavailable, record a continuity handover for unresolved high-priority work. Managers should review coverage gaps, open high/critical handovers and upcoming field/event commitments. Do not place medical diagnoses or unnecessary HR-sensitive details in operational handover notes.
