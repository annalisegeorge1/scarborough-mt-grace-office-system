# Correspondence & Templates

The Correspondence Centre provides controlled starting templates for common public-service correspondence and a training workflow for Draft, Ready for Review, Approved and Issued states.

## Production controls

- Official reference numbers must be assigned centrally.
- Issued correspondence must record the authenticated staff user, approval/signatory and immutable issue timestamp.
- Final files must be stored in private document storage and linked to the Records Centre.
- Templates should be versioned and approved by management.
- A template must never substitute for checking the factual circumstances of the individual matter.
- Resident personal information must not be copied into unrelated correspondence or public website content.

## Recommended workflow

Draft → Ready for Review → Approved → Issued → Registered in Records Centre.
