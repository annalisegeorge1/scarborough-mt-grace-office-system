Scarborough / Mt. Grace District Office — V232

FORM + LANDSCAPE STABILITY PASS

V232 is a screenshot-driven correction pass based on the October 2 tablet screenshots supplied after V231.

PRIMARY ISSUE FOUND
The focused enquiry page was still inheriting the original legacy contact layout:

.contact-grid { grid-template-columns: 2fr 1fr; }

That layout made sense when the section contained two independent contact columns.
The modern enquiry section now contains one full resident form workflow.

On tablet landscape this legacy rule could place the enquiry form/review content into a narrow column, producing:
- large empty space on the left
- extremely narrow form/review cards on the right
- labels breaking letter-by-letter
- Supporting Documents and Review Your Details becoming difficult to use

V232 removes that conflict.

1. Enquiry becomes a true single-column workflow
#enquiry .contact-grid now uses:
- one minmax(0,1fr) column
- full-width content children
- full-width form-area
- full-width district-enquiry-form

The section label, process strip, form area and section-end control all occupy the full available row.

2. Useful form columns preserved
Once the form itself owns the full page width:
- screens >=901px use two form-field columns
- wide review panels can use three summary columns
- medium tablets use two review columns
- phones use one column

This uses screen width without crushing the controls.

3. Full-row form content
Long/structural form elements span the whole form width:
- Message textarea
- Resident Update Preferences
- Community note
- Supporting Documents
- Review Your Details
- submission/consent area

4. Anti-crush text protection
Enquiry cards, stages, review values, upload guidance and notification copy receive explicit:
- min-width:0
- normal word breaking
- safe overflow wrapping

This prevents labels such as:
BEST CONTACT
PREFERRED CONTACT
COMMUNITY AREA

from collapsing into vertical letter stacks.

5. Supporting Documents treatment
The currently disabled upload field is visually presented as intentionally unavailable rather than looking broken.

Dark mode receives its own disabled-upload treatment.

6. Resident Update Preferences
The V138 notification-preference panel receives dark-mode surfaces and readable dark-mode copy.

No preference behavior or notification logic is changed.

7. Touch-landscape form use
On coarse-pointer landscape:
- focused Forms / Enquiry uses the full available width
- internal wrap width limits are removed
- form padding becomes responsive
- the four enquiry process stages can display as four usable columns where space allows

8. Dark Programmes & Assistance chips
The supplied dark screenshot showed the “I need help with…” programme buttons with very low contrast.

V232 gives dark-mode programme buttons:
- dark slate surface
- light readable text
- brass-aware border
- clear hover/focus state

No programme wording or eligibility guidance is changed.

9. Touch-landscape top navigation
An older V27 rule explicitly hid navigation buttons 5 onward at <=1100 CSS pixels.

V232 overrides that rule on touch-landscape so:
- Forms
- Updates
- Contact
- Explore

remain reachable.

The main navigation row horizontally scrolls when necessary.

10. Compact search on landscape tablets
The site search now occupies an icon-sized slot when not focused on touch-landscape.

When focused:
- it expands to a usable search field
- search behavior remains unchanged
- result dropdown remains available

This gives the main navigation buttons more room.

11. Smart Next Step landscape density
V226 Smart Next Step becomes more compact in touch-landscape:
- reduced padding/height
- contextual copy remains
- action row stays horizontally accessible
- no action is removed

12. Services launchers
On medium portrait/tablet widths, V228 Readiness and V231 Service Intelligence launchers become more compact:
- two-column internal layout
- supporting paragraph hidden
- action remains visible
- all functionality preserved

On very narrow phones the existing stacked pattern remains.

13. Floating-control collision safety
On short touch-landscape screens:
- Explore stays compact
- accessibility control remains clear of content
- the legacy right contact rail is slightly reduced on focused Forms pages
