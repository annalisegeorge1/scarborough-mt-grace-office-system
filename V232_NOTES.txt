Scarborough / Mt. Grace District Office — V232

FORM RESCUE + OVERLAY DISCIPLINE

V232 is a screenshot-driven correction pass based on the October 2 tablet captures showing the enquiry form collapsing into a narrow right-hand column and several remaining fixed-control/navigation issues.

1. Root enquiry-layout correction
The enquiry section inherited the original .contact-grid layout:
- desktop: 2fr / 1fr
- mobile: 1fr

That structure was created for an older, simpler contact layout. The current enquiry section now contains:
- section label
- four-step process strip
- enquiry action choices
- tracking guidance
- privacy guidance
- resident process cards
- full enquiry form
- supporting document note
- review details
- submit controls
- section-end control

Keeping that modern content inside the old two-column auto-placement system allowed the form/review area to collapse into a narrow column on landscape tablets.

V232 changes #enquiry .contact-grid to one stable content column at all screen sizes.

2. Form width preservation
V232 explicitly sets:
- #enquiry .form-area to a centered full-width content frame
- #district-enquiry-form to full width
- all fields/controls to min-width:0 and max-width:100%
- modern form grid to repeat(2,minmax(0,1fr)) where space permits

This prevents controls from forcing or inheriting narrow fixed widths.

3. Full-width form blocks
The following now span both field columns:
- textarea/message area
- Resident Update Preferences
- Supporting Documents status/note
- Community-area explanatory note
- Review Your Details panel
- final form action/submission block

4. Review Details correction
Review Your Details remains a two-column grid on wider screens and becomes one column on small screens.

Labels and values use safe wrapping rather than letter-by-letter breaking.

5. Small-screen form behavior
At <=760px:
- field grid becomes one column
- review grid becomes one column
- form-area padding is reduced appropriately

6. Touch-landscape form behavior
On coarse-pointer landscape devices:
- enquiry wrapper receives safe horizontal padding
- form area can use up to 1180px while remaining fluid
- form fields remain two columns where room exists
- review panel remains two columns

7. Programme help-chip contrast
The “I need help with…” chips inside Resident Programmes sit on a permanently dark editorial section.

V232 forces those chip labels to remain light and readable in both normal and v112 dark mode.

8. Touch-landscape navigation
The Spanish shortcut was dynamically injected with absolute desktop positioning and could overlap the Scarborough / Mt. Grace brand lockup.

In touch-landscape V232 now:
- moves ESPAÑOL into normal nav flow
- places it near the end of the swipe rail
- reduces its footprint
- keeps navigation buttons horizontally scrollable
- reduces search width
- keeps later destinations reachable
- protects the brand lockup from overlap

9. Fixed control discipline
On <=900px:
- main content receives bottom clearance for the fixed resident dock
- accessibility controls are raised above the fixed dock
- Explore is raised to a consistent dock-safe position

On short touch-landscape screens:
- Explore is reduced slightly
- accessibility control is reduced slightly

10. Focused Forms / Contact safeguards
When #enquiry appears inside the focused Forms or Contact route:
- #enquiry itself cannot inherit a narrow max-width
- its .wrap cannot inherit a narrow max-width
- the form remains full-width inside the V204/V230 focused layout

11. Preservation
V232 is CSS-only.

No changes to:
- enquiry submission JavaScript
