Audit Review

Review the controls required to create a reliable history of sign-ins, case changes, publishing actions, document access and permission changes.

AUDIT REQUIREMENTAppend-only

Production events must not be editable by ordinary staff.

RETAINPolicy-led

Retention period must be approved before launch.

CAPTUREWho + What

Actor, action, object, time, outcome and source.

SENSITIVE DATAMinimise

Do not copy case contents into audit messages.

Production rules

  • Write audit events on the server, not from browser JavaScript.
  • Ordinary staff cannot delete or alter events.
  • Never log passwords, session tokens, full medical details or uploaded-document contents.
  • Record export, publication, permission and deletion actions.
  • Protect audit access as a privileged permission.
Production security must be enforced by the server. Browser controls are administrative aids, not security boundaries.
