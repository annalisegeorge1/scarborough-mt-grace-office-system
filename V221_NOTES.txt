Scarborough / Mt. Grace District Office — V221

DARK-MODE CONSISTENCY + DESKTOP/TABLET REFINEMENT

V221 extends the public design system into dark mode and improves wider-screen spacing without removing information or changing staff functionality.

1. Focused-page dark mode
- Dark-mode focused pages now use a consistent deep navy canvas instead of mixing bright modern components with the older dark theme.
- Route identity strips become dark civic panels with white copy and muted brass accents.
- Quick-action cards use dark layered surfaces with readable white/slate copy.
- The desktop side navigator receives a full dark treatment, including active states, side actions and WhatsApp styling.
- Existing large legacy source sections remain readable and authoritative.

2. Progressive-disclosure dark mode
- V208 “More guidance & service information” and V209 “Explore more” hubs now have proper dark surfaces.
- Buttons, expanded states, counters and descriptive copy retain accessible contrast.

3. District Digital Feed / Community Stream
- Feed shell, filters, search, feed actions, metadata, “new” banner and footer are now visually consistent in dark mode.
- Pinned/new/thread functionality remains unchanged.
- Feed items retain full source links and statuses.

4. Progress threads
- V215 public progress histories now have dedicated dark-mode surfaces.
- Dates, statuses, summaries and notes remain readable while keeping the existing history structure.

5. Homepage digital additions
- V211 District Office / Community gateway cards and V213 District Pulse now follow the selected dark theme.
- The homepage keeps its existing content and routes.

6. Explore drawer
- Search, groups, active items and current-route treatment now have a consistent dark-mode palette.
- V218 one-group mobile accordion behaviour remains unchanged.

7. Enquiry form
- Inputs, select fields, textareas, placeholders, labels and message counter now have explicit dark-mode styling.
- V220 submission and keyboard behaviour is unchanged.
- No form data is stored by V221.

8. Desktop/tablet refinement
- Focused pages receive slightly more deliberate desktop vertical spacing.
- Hover-capable desktops get restrained lift/transition polish.
- 901–1160px layouts use a narrower side navigator and tighter gap so the main content is not squeezed.
- Route identity principles stack more naturally on medium laptop/tablet widths.
- Large desktops (1360px+) receive a slightly wider 1500px page frame, 318px side navigator and more breathing room without stretching content excessively.

9. Browser chrome theme
- V221 observes the existing v112-dark class.
- Browser theme-color is synchronized to the selected light/dark appearance.
- color-scheme metadata is provided for compatible native controls.
- Existing theme preference/toggle logic remains authoritative; V221 does not introduce a second theme preference.

Preservation
- No public information removed.
- No navigation destinations removed.
- No staff CMS changes.
- No staff workflow changes.
- No V215 progress-thread changes.
- No enquiry endpoint, tracker, authentication, database or publishing changes.
