# Pilot Operations Runbook

## Purpose
Use a limited pilot to prove real operating workflows before wider production use.

## Recommended pilot scope
- A small named group of trained staff.
- A limited set of services and workflows.
- A defined start/end period.
- Daily management review.
- A documented rollback version.

## Daily pilot routine
1. Confirm system health, authentication and backup status.
2. Review overnight incidents and unresolved high-risk items.
3. Check case/referral/appointment queues and public publishing tasks.
4. Record new incidents and change requests in the Pilot Centre.
5. Do not implement high-risk changes without approval and rollback planning.
6. At close of day, reconcile unresolved matters and confirm no silent data loss.

## Expansion rule
Do not expand the pilot while a Critical incident remains unresolved. High-severity incidents require resolution or formally approved mitigation before expansion.

## Rollback
If resident confidentiality, data integrity, authentication or core case tracking cannot be trusted, stop the affected workflow and revert to the last approved release. Record the reason and recovery actions.
