Scarborough / Mt. Grace District Office — V213

DISTRICT DIGITAL FEED

V213 adds a modern short-form public-information layer inspired by the clarity and immediacy of microblogging systems, while preserving every existing source section and the District Office civic/editorial design.

Core rule:
- No existing information is removed.
- The feed is a summary/navigation layer, not a replacement for full source content.
- Every feed item links back to its complete source section.

Updates page:
- Adds a full District Digital Feed near the top of /updates/.
- Pulls from:
  * Staff-published public updates
  * District Activity Hub notices
  * Tracked projects and follow-up matters
  * What We Heard community matters
  * Public Action & Progress
  * Community Gallery
- Staff-published items with dates are prioritised by date.
- Undated current public information remains grouped by source/priority rather than receiving invented timestamps.
- Adds category filters:
  * All
  * Office updates
  * Notices
  * Projects
  * Community
  * Progress
- Adds in-feed search.
- Adds status pills using existing public statuses such as Confirmed, Planned, In Development and Follow-up Required.
- Adds location/context metadata when already available in the source.
- Adds:
  * Open full information
  * Copy link
  * Share
- Native mobile sharing is used when supported; Copy link remains available everywhere.
- Live staff-published content is watched with MutationObserver so newly rendered public items automatically appear in the feed.

Community page:
- Adds a Community Stream using the same digital system.
- Limits the stream to notices, projects and community information.
- Keeps the full Community page content and Explore More hierarchy below it.
- Links back to the full underlying section for each item.

Homepage:
- Adds a compact District Pulse immediately after the District Activity Hub.
- Shows up to four current public items.
- Keeps the V211 chapter narrative intact.
- Links each preview into the correct focused public section.
- Automatically refreshes if staff-published updates appear after page load.

Existing live update cards:
- Remain fully present.
- Receive a visual refresh so they match the new feed language.
- The original publication workflow and public-content runtime are not replaced.

Information integrity:
- V213 does not invent dates.
- V213 does not imply an agency completed work when the source only records District Office advocacy/follow-up.
- Existing status wording is preserved.
- Repeated exact titles are de-duplicated in the feed, but the original source sections remain untouched.
- Empty public-plan/public-matter states are not turned into fake feed posts.
- Gallery content remains contextualised as community/District Office imagery rather than evidence of an event unless the source states otherwise.

Accessibility and interaction:
- Feed filters are keyboard-accessible buttons.
- Search has an accessible hidden label and visible placeholder.
- Feed results use semantic article markup and aria-live updates.
- Copy/share actions work without removing access to the source.
- Reduced-motion preferences remain respected through the site-wide styling system.
