CONTENT HIERARCHY / EXPANDABLE SUPPORTING INFORMATION

V208 reduces page length on the two most task-heavy public routes: Resident Services and Forms & Portals.

Resident Services:
Primary content remains visible:
- Start Here
- Services & Assistance
- Programmes

Supporting information becomes expandable:
- Public Service Paths
- Service Finder
- Top Resident Tasks
- Service Overview
- Quick Route
- How We Help
- Help Selector
- Support Areas
- Resident Guide
- Frequently Asked Questions
- Resources

Forms & Portals:
Primary content remains visible:
- Portal Hub
- Service Forms
- Submit an Enquiry

Supporting information becomes expandable:
- Documents
- Case Tracking Guide
- Resident Journey

Behaviour:
- Supporting sections start collapsed.
- Residents can open only the guidance they need.
- Multiple supporting sections may be open at the same time.
- "Collapse all" closes all supporting sections.
- Existing deep links remain functional.
- Sidebar links that point to collapsed supporting content reveal that content before scrolling to it.
- Supporting sections are removed from the always-visible right-side page index to keep that index concise.
- Primary sections are reordered to appear immediately after the page quick actions.

Accessibility:
- Expand controls expose aria-expanded and aria-controls.
- Collapsed content uses aria-hidden.
- Reduced-motion preferences are respected.
