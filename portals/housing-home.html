Housing & Home Support Portal

A starting point for housing, home-repair and related household-support enquiries.

What the office can do

Help document the concern, identify the appropriate agency or programme, prepare a referral where appropriate, and follow up on the office-side record.

Useful preparation

A clear description of the housing/home concern, contact information, address/location and any documents specifically requested by the responsible programme.

Important

The District Office does not determine programme eligibility or approval.

Need help choosing the right path?

The District Office can help residents understand where to start, what information may be needed, and how to follow up. The responsible agency or programme makes eligibility and approval decisions.
