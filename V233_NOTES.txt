Scarborough / Mt. Grace District Office — V233

SCREENSHOT POLISH

V233 completes the remaining screenshot-driven fixes after the V232 form-stability pass.

1. Programme help-chip contrast
The “I need help with…” items inside Resident Programmes are spans, not buttons.

V232 improved programme buttons, but those exact chips could remain low-contrast.

V233 gives the chips explicit readable treatment in:
- light mode
- dark mode

No programme wording or eligibility information is changed.

2. Spanish navigation shortcut
The Spanish shortcut is dynamically injected by public-content-runtime with absolute desktop positioning.

On touch-landscape tablets it could overlap the Scarborough / Mt. Grace brand lockup.

V233 moves ESPAÑOL into normal navigation flow in touch landscape:
- static positioning
- normal rail order
- compact pill sizing
- no brand overlap
- remains horizontally reachable with the rest of the navigation

3. Portrait mobile dock clearance
The fixed resident dock remains available, but V233 reduces crowding around it.

On portrait screens <=900px:
- Explore sits above the dock
- accessibility controls sit above the dock
- footer gains bottom clearance
- mobile dock padding is reduced slightly
- mobile dock action height is reduced slightly
- scroll padding accounts for the fixed resident dock

4. Short portrait screens
On shorter portrait displays:
- Explore scales down slightly
- accessibility controls scale down slightly

5. Services tool-stack spacing
V228 Resident Readiness and V231 Service Intelligence remain fully available.

Their spacing is tightened on smaller screens so the Services page does not feel like consecutive oversized utility panels.

No launcher or feature is removed.

6. Modal/sheet coexistence
Explore and accessibility floating controls recede while the following are open:
- V224 Command Center
- V225 Resident Journey
- V228 Resident Readiness
- V231 Service Intelligence

This reduces visual overlap and competing controls.
