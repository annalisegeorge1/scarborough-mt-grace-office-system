# Resident Feedback, Complaints & Service Recovery

## Purpose
The resident-experience workflow records feedback about District Office service and separates that feedback from eligibility decisions or external-agency outcomes.

## Minimum workflow
1. Record feedback or complaint with the minimum necessary information.
2. Acknowledge complaints promptly.
3. Assign an owner and due date when service recovery is required.
4. Record the outcome/evidence.
5. Review recurring themes in the Quality Centre.
6. Escalate serious privacy, safety, discrimination, fraud or security concerns through the appropriate incident/governance process.

## Safeguards
- Do not collect political affiliation or voting preference.
- Complaints must not disadvantage a resident seeking assistance.
- Do not expose complaint details on the public website.
- Production records require authenticated server storage, audit logging and retention rules.
- Satisfaction measures must distinguish District Office service from decisions made by external authorities.
