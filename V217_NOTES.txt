Scarborough / Mt. Grace District Office — V217

SCREENSHOT-DRIVEN MOBILE POLISH

V217 responds to the second live screenshot set after V216 and fixes remaining legacy-style conflicts without removing public information or staff functionality.

Issues identified from screenshots:

1. Empty navy masthead band on mobile/tablet
Cause:
- The legacy desktop navigation container remained visible on <=900px layouts even though the dedicated V125 mobile navigation rail was already present.
- Its desktop link row was hidden, leaving a mostly empty navy block.
Correction:
- The legacy desktop nav is hidden only at <=900px.
- The dedicated mobile navigation rail remains fully available.
- Desktop navigation remains unchanged.

2. Explore drawer active child links still white-on-pale
Observed:
- Contact Us
- Resident Guide
- Community Action Centre
and similar active child links displayed pale backgrounds with pale/white text.
Correction:
- Active V202 sub-links now receive explicit navy text, blue active rule and dark bullet.
- Parent groups containing an active child receive readable navy/grey text and restrained brass emphasis.
- Existing open/close/search behaviour is unchanged.

3. Resident Programmes four-step guidance contrast
Observed:
- Identify your need
- Read the source document
- Prepare your documents
- Contact us if unsure
were still using white/dim copy designed for a dark V17 background while the focused page now presents them on a pale panel.
Correction:
- Step cards become clean white/paper cards with navy headings and readable slate descriptions.
- Gold numbered circles remain.
- The programme disclaimer also receives readable dark copy on a light panel.
- No programme guidance text is removed or changed.

4. District Office Team dark-section contrast
Observed:
- District Office Team banner text and the compact-directory description were too dark against navy.
- The team section’s introductory labels were partially disappearing.
Correction:
- District Office Team badge, banner, directory description, intro label and group headings receive explicit light/navy-section contrast.
- “Meet the people behind the office” retains the stronger muted-gold editorial treatment.
- White staff cards retain dark text; the fix does not turn all team content white.

5. Footer vertical looseness
Observed:
- Legacy mobile footer bottom padding and newer utility-link padding stacked together.
- This created a large empty navy area above the persistent mobile dock.
Correction:
- One controlled safe area now handles the dock clearance.
- Footer secondary links use compact bottom padding.
- Footer watermark is smaller, fainter and positioned closer to the bottom.
- No footer information or links are removed.

6. Fixed mobile controls
Observed:
- Call / WhatsApp / Enquiry / Track remained visually tall on the tablet/phone capture.
- The 100% text-size badge was peeking from behind the Explore button.
Correction:
- Persistent action dock is reduced while retaining usable tap areas.
- Explore FAB is slightly tighter.
- Accessibility controls move to the lower right above the dock instead of underneath Explore.
- Text-size controls remain fully available.

7. Mobile navigation density
- V125 arrows and pills are slightly smaller.
- Scroll padding is added so edge items feel less clipped.
- All Home / Office / Services / Community / Forms / Updates / Contact / Explore destinations remain available.

Responsive scope:
- Main corrections align with the site’s <=900px mobile/tablet breakpoint rather than only <=760px.
- This better matches the actual device layout visible in the supplied screenshots.

Preservation:
- No public content removed.
- No navigation destination removed.
- No staff CMS changes.
- No V215 progress-thread changes.
- No forms, enquiry, tracking, authentication, database or publishing changes.
- V217 is CSS-only.
