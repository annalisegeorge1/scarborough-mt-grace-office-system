Scarborough / Mt. Grace District Office — V210

AESTHETIC RESTORATION

V210 restores more of the visual language that made the earlier public site feel distinctive, while preserving the clearer V202–V209 information architecture.

Restored visual ideas:
- Deep navy editorial panels and layered civic gradients.
- Georgia/serif emphasis for important headings and action titles.
- Concentric civic rings and seal-like medallions.
- Fine constellation/orbit linework.
- Restrained metallic/brass details instead of bright yellow accents.
- Layered radial backgrounds and subtle paper-like surfaces.
- Stronger image/showcase framing.
- More deliberate visual rhythm between large content blocks.
- A stronger connection between the focused pages and the original public homepage aesthetic.

Focused-page changes:
- All focused-page heroes return to a unified navy/blue District Office foundation.
- Route identities remain visible through accent details rather than replacing the core brand with unrelated full-page colours.
- Route identity initials are restyled as circular civic medallions.
- Quick-action cards gain layered materials, serif labels and fine metallic rules.
- Content cards gain atmospheric radial layers and decorative constellation ornaments.
- Original v181 constellation signatures are restored to a more visible level after being heavily muted in V204.
- Sticky page navigation gains deeper navy framing and subtle civic-ring detailing.
- V208/V209 expandable information panels are visually integrated into the same editorial language.
- Gallery/showcase areas regain a darker, more dramatic framed presentation.
- Small editorial dividers, folio numbers and an SMG summary seal improve visual continuity without changing content.

Design restraint:
- Metallic details use muted brass/champagne tones rather than strong yellow.
- Decorative ornaments disappear or simplify on small screens.
- Existing page structure, hierarchy, deep links, enquiry logic, staff authentication and public content remain unchanged.
