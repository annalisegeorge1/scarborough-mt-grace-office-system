# Meetings, Events & Attendance Operations

## Purpose
Create a consistent operational record for Town Halls, community meetings, stakeholder roundtables, NGO/partner meetings, agency meetings, staff meetings and public events.

## Core workflow
Planning → Invitations/Outreach → Confirmed → Held → Actions in Follow-up → Closed.

## Privacy
Attendance names, contact details and internal minutes are staff records. They must not be automatically published. Public website updates must use a separately prepared resident-safe outcome summary and the existing CMS approval workflow.

## Action management
Every decision that requires work should have an owner, due date and status. Overdue actions should surface through the operational attention/reminder system once connected to the production backend.

## Production requirements
The production service should store meetings, attendees, agenda items, decisions and actions in the central database, enforce role permissions, write audit events, support secure document attachments, and expose only approved public summaries to the website CMS.
