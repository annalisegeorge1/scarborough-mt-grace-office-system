# Applications, Referrals & Assistance

This module records the District Office’s administrative assistance to residents seeking services from external Divisions, agencies or programmes.

## Core rule
The office may explain requirements, help prepare documentation, make referrals/submissions, record acknowledgements and follow up. It must not represent that it determines eligibility or guarantees approval.

## Production requirements
- central server-generated application references;
- authenticated staff ownership and audit history;
- secure links to supporting documents in the Records Centre;
- agency/reference tracking and follow-up scheduling;
- clear outcome source and decision date;
- resident-safe status text separated from internal notes;
- no collection of political affiliation, voting preference or campaign data in service workflows.
